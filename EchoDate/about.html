ECHØ DATE is an indie rock band from western Germany. Their sound blends diverse influences into a melancholic and atmospheric soundscape. Reflective lyrics meet melodic basslines, driving drums, and textured guitars, creating a dynamic yet cohesive style. Shaped by each member’s individuality, their songs evolve organically, drifting into new directions while maintaining a distinct emotional core.
